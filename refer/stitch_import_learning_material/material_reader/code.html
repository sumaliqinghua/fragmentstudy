Renewable energy is collected from resources that are naturally replenished on a human timescale. It includes sources such as sunlight, wind, rain, tides, waves, and geothermal heat.

Although most renewable energy sources are sustainable, some are not.

For example, some biomass sources are considered unsustainable at current rates of exploitation. About 20% of humans' global energy consumption comes from renewables, including nearly 30% of electricity.

This share is rising rapidly as countries move away from fossil fuels to combat climate change. While many renewable energy projects are large-scale, renewable technologies are also suited to rural and remote areas.
